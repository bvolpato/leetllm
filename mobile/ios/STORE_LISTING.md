## App Description (Max 4,000 characters)

Master the skills needed to design, train, and deploy production-grade Artificial Intelligence and Large Language Model (LLM) systems. LeetLLM is a progressive, curriculum-driven learning platform built specifically for software developers, ML engineers, and computer science students.

Unlike passive tutorials, LeetLLM focuses on active, hands-on understanding. Learn how transformer architectures function from the ground up, implement backpropagation from scratch, optimize tensor shapes in PyTorch, and master distributed LLM serving.

### Key Features:

- **197 Progressive Lessons:** Follow a 15-phase roadmap covering AI foundations, Bayesian inference, causal learning, differential privacy, PyTorch, retrieval, agents, inference, and production systems.
- **Mac & iPad Desktop Experience:** Seamlessly downloadable on Apple Silicon Macs and iPads with multi-column navigation, resizable windows, and full keyboard shortcuts (⌘1–⌘5 navigation, text size controls).
- **Interactive Quizzes & Scoring:** Reinforce your understanding with active quiz challenges. The app tracks your attempts and saves your best scores.
- **Fully Offline-First:** Study and read lessons, review diagrams, and test yourself anywhere. The entire curriculum and all interactive resources are fully pre-bundled inside the app.
- **757 AI & ML Glossary Terms:** Search research concepts, engineering terminology, worked examples, architectural definitions, and related lessons without an internet connection.
- **47 Coding Practice Problems:** Browse exercise summaries on your device, then open the full interactive coding environment on the web.
- **Progress Syncing:** Log in securely via email to sync your read history and quiz accomplishments across devices, or use the app completely as a Guest.

### Curriculum Covered:

1. **AI Foundations:** NumPy tensor shapes, broadcasting, data structures, and probability.
2. **Machine Learning from Scratch:** Backpropagation mechanics, linear regression, gradient descent, and PyTorch training loops.
3. **Core LLM Architectures:** Transformer self-attention, tokenizers, decoding algorithms, and beam search.
4. **Production System Design:** Feature pipelines, ranking, forecasting, model monitoring, and A/B testing.
5. **Advanced Lab Builds:** Shipping document QA products, fine-tuning classifiers, deploying evaluation dashboards, and building production LLM agents.

Take control of your AI engineering path. Build implementation speed, master the underlying math, and gain the credentials to deploy production-grade machine learning systems.

## Notes for the App Reviewer

- **Authentication / Login:** Users can explore the app fully in **Guest Mode** without signing up. However, the app supports progress syncing via Email Authentication.
- **Reviewer Credentials:**
  - The curriculum, glossary, learning tracks, quizzes, and local progress work immediately without an account. Optional synchronization supports email/password, Google, and Sign in with Apple when the corresponding providers are enabled.
- **Account Deletion:** In compliance with App Store Guidelines, the app contains an explicit "Delete Account & Synced Progress" button under the **Account** tab when a user is signed in. This deletes all Supabase profile data and authentication details permanently.
