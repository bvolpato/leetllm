## Description

<en-US>
LeetLLM is the ultimate companion for developers, researchers, and engineers looking to master Large Language Models (LLMs) and advanced AI engineering. Designed as an offline-first, highly detailed educational resource, LeetLLM offers a structured path through the entire lifecycle of modern AI systems.

Whether you are preparing for technical interviews, specializing in AI development, or building production-grade LLM applications, LeetLLM provides the deep technical intuition and hands-on conceptual practice you need.

KEY FEATURES:

• 197 Detailed Lessons — A comprehensive curriculum spanning 15 roadmap phases, covering computing foundations, math, neural networks, Transformers, fine-tuning, RAG, agents, LLMOps, and responsible AI.
• Structured Learning Tracks — Follow focused learning paths designed for specific outcomes (e.g., Inference and Serving, Core LLM Foundations) with progress bars and reading estimates.
• Interactive Theory Quizzes — Test your understanding of core concepts with multiple-choice questions, detailed explanations, and score tracking.
• Lesson Mastery Checks — Check your comprehension at the end of every lesson to mark it as complete.
• Offline-First Design — All lessons, diagrams, math formulas (LaTeX), and glossary entries are fully bundled. Access everything without an active internet connection.
• Supabase Sync — Sign in with your LeetLLM account to sync your reading progress and quiz achievements across web and mobile devices.
• Comprehensive Glossary — Hundreds of technical terms defined with intuitions, formulas, decision rules, code snippets, and common pitfalls.
• Custom Reader Settings — Tailor your reading experience with serif or sans-serif fonts, adjustable text sizes, and system/light/dark appearance modes.
</en-US>
