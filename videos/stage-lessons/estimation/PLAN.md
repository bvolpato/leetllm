## The job of each color

| Color | Job |
|---|---|
| `pink` | A message that needs a specialist, and the true share of a queue. |
| `blue` | The sample of 100 labels: a sampled message, and what comes from 100 labels (its estimate, its cloud, its Wald bar). |
| `cyan` | The larger sample of 400 labels. |
| `green` | Rates that the data accept: a Wilson interval, an interval that covers, a choice that passes. |
| `red` | A failure: a collapsed interval, an interval that misses, a biased estimate. |
| `yellow` | What the voice points at now: the candidate, the 1.96 cutoff, a result at the moment that it lands. |
| `ink`, `dim` | The 20% line and the text. Quiet parts, unknown labels, prior terms (outlines). |
