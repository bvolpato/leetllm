- [RFC 9110 If-Match](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html#section-13.1.1) uses strong comparison. Hash one validated framed envelope, with agreed bytes, and bind it through protected decision storage or verified signatures. A digest alone authenticates nobody and doesn't inspect live state.
