The article's eight validation scores (displayed to three decimals) and labels are shown on a shared score axis comparing cuts at 0.50 and 0.20 without changing weights. Lowering the cut adds V8 (required review, score 0.244) and V6 (unnecessary review, score 0.325). At 0.50: TP=3, FP=1, FN=1, TN=3, cost 120+18=138 dollars. At 0.20: TP=4, FP=2, FN=0, TN=2, cost 2*18=36 dollars under the $120 missed-error and $18 verification costs.
