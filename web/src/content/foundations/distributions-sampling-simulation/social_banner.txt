The article's theoretical Poisson(2.2) and 88% Poisson(1.2) + 12% Poisson(10) models have means 2.2 and 2.256. Each PMF is plotted at counts 0-5, plus one grouped >5 category, on matching probability axes. Tail probabilities are calculated from the models, not taken from finite sample outputs or presented as production metrics. No external image service or upload is required.
