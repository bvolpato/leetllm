The article's 16/100, 160/1000, and 1600/10000 examples use two-sided Wilson intervals with z=1.96 on a shared 0-30% axis. The source calculates endpoints; dots mark observed fractions, not Wilson centers. The confidence intervals describe sampling uncertainty under the binomial model, not posterior probability or protection against sampling bias. No external image service or upload is required.
