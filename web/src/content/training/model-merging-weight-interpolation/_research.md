# Model merging: manual evidence review

Reviewed September 22, 2026. The prior generated packet contained incorrect claims about tokenizer compatibility, TIES majority voting, low-magnitude updates, and DARE output expectations. This note records manually checked sources and their limits.

- **Loss barriers:** Entezari et al. define a barrier above the coefficient-dependent endpoint chord. Frankle et al. use mean endpoint error instead. Zero chord barrier doesn't establish convexity of the path or a multidimensional basin. The lesson includes an authored polynomial counterexample. Sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2110.06296 and https://arxiv.org/html/1912.05671
- **Permutation symmetry:** Permuting a hidden layer requires corresponding changes to incoming weights, biases, and outgoing weights. The activation must commute with that permutation. Git Re-Basin's global multilayer weight-matching formulation is generally hard; coordinate updates solve individual assignments with other permutations fixed. Activation matching is a separate method. The illustrated two-unit permutation is known by construction, not a solver result. Sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2209.04836 and https://arxiv.org/html/2110.06296
- **Soups:** Uniform and greedy soups average checkpoints without another gradient run. The original greedy rule accepts ties; the lesson's helper requires strict improvement. Learned soups optimize coefficients with gradients. Angles analyzed in the paper are fine-tuning-delta angles, not proof that absolute same-base weights are orthogonal. Improvements on a chosen metric don't imply improvements on every distribution. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2203.05482
- **Task negation:** The original GPT-2 Large experiment reports toxicity 4.8% to 0.8% and WikiText-103 perplexity 16.4 to 16.9. This is an evaluated behavior change, not a certified safety or training-data deletion result. Deltas needn't isolate unique skills. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2212.04089
- **LoRA:** Effective updates are scaled products B A. Independently averaging factors introduces cross terms. The lesson's two-matrix fixture demonstrates exact concatenation and a rank-two sum of rank-one updates. Lower-rank compression is approximate. No PEFT API was executed. Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685 and https://huggingface.co/docs/peft/developer_guides/model_merging
- **TIES:** Trim by magnitude, elect sign using signed sum, and average aligned survivors. Small magnitude doesn't identify irrelevant parameters. The paper's PEFT analysis concerns its evaluated models/tasks, not a universal 20% recipe. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2306.01708
- **DARE:** Bernoulli keep masks plus fixed 1/(1-p) rescaling preserve expected delta coordinates. The paper's fixed-linear-transformation argument doesn't certify deep-network outputs. At p=0.9, initially nonzero independent deltas overlap in 1% of all coordinates and 5.3% of active coordinates. WizardMath-70B at 99% dropping and WizardLM-70B's AlpacaEval degradation at 90% dropping rule out a parameter-count-only guarantee. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2311.03099
- **SLERP:** Shoemake's graphics paper establishes quaternion interpolation geometry. The displayed vector formula has constant radius for equal-norm, nonzero endpoints away from parallel/antipodal cases. It isn't an empirical neural-network quality guarantee. The actual fourth PDF page was rendered and read. Source: https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~kiranb/animation/p245-shoemake.pdf

## Implementation snapshot

Mergekit source commit `9eeb539892c6753b032ea164e818b44a8c7a401b`, checked September 22, 2026:

- Realized-L1 rescaling differs from the paper's fixed inverse keep probability. A zero mask stays zero; the authored enumeration shows the library convention needn't be unbiased. Source: https://github.com/arcee-ai/mergekit/blob/9eeb539892c6753b032ea164e818b44a8c7a401b/mergekit/sparsify.py
- Source weights enter before sign election. Sum-based election chooses positive at zero signed sum. Normalization divides by aligned contributor weights; lambda scales the final delta. Source: https://github.com/arcee-ai/mergekit/blob/9eeb539892c6753b032ea164e818b44a8c7a401b/mergekit/merge_methods/generalized_task_arithmetic.py
- DARE-TIES defaults to normalize=false and rescale=true. Source: https://github.com/arcee-ai/mergekit/blob/9eeb539892c6753b032ea164e818b44a8c7a401b/mergekit/merge_methods/registry.py
- Pairwise SLERP uses two total inputs including its designated base. It merges individual tensors and linearly falls back for nearly parallel/antipodal directions. The lesson's explicit zero-vector fallback differs from library small-norm handling. Source: https://github.com/arcee-ai/mergekit/blob/9eeb539892c6753b032ea164e818b44a8c7a401b/mergekit/merge_methods/slerp.py
- dtype casts merge inputs; out_dtype casts outputs afterward. Union vocabulary retains the base tokenization pipeline, and auto chat-template selection doesn't reconcile formats. Source: https://github.com/arcee-ai/mergekit/blob/9eeb539892c6753b032ea164e818b44a8c7a401b/README.md
- Token-string mappings align source IDs. Failed input tokenizer loading can warn and assume the base, which needs manual investigation. Vocabulary-axis tensors use available donor fallbacks, not necessarily random initialization. Sources: https://github.com/arcee-ai/mergekit/blob/9eeb539892c6753b032ea164e818b44a8c7a401b/mergekit/tokenizer/build.py and https://github.com/arcee-ai/mergekit/blob/9eeb539892c6753b032ea164e818b44a8c7a401b/mergekit/tokenizer/embed.py
- The CLI option generator turns random_seed into --random-seed. A recorded seed alone doesn't promise reproducibility across implementation/hardware changes. Source: https://github.com/arcee-ai/mergekit/blob/9eeb539892c6753b032ea164e818b44a8c7a401b/mergekit/options.py

## Search and release limits

- Sakana's original parameter-space and data-flow experiments use CMA-ES over recipe parameters. The EvoLLM-JP search and final MGSM evaluation use disjoint examples. Historical results aren't current rankings. Sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2403.13187v1 and https://sakana.ai/evolutionary-model-merge/
- The lesson's release scores, curves, and one-percentage-point margin are authored policy fixtures. Missing scores/baselines and nonfinite values fail their applicable gates. An absolute pass can still violate a relative regression margin. No real-model benchmark, model download, full-model merge, GPU inference, or evolutionary search was executed.
