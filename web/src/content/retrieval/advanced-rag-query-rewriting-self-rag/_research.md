# Manual evidence review, 2026-09-22

Full article, metadata, ten questions, examples, four native sources, banner source/provenance, and Mermaid definition read individually. These notes replace a generated packet with unsupported industry accuracy percentages and incorrect claims about hallucination removal and CRAG's ambiguous route.

- Query rewriting: Ma et al. train a T5-large rewriter with reader feedback; a conversational standalone-query helper is an application variant, not reproduction of their experiments. Primary paper: https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.322.pdf
- RRF: the original formula sums reciprocal one-based ranks; the pilot fixed k=60. A missing truncated-list result contributes zero in this implementation. Repeated variants can bias fusion; rank fusion is not verification or the only possible score-fusion method. Primary paper: https://plg.uwaterloo.ca/~gvcormac/cormacksigir09-rrf.pdf
- RAG-Fusion pairs generated variants with RRF. It supplies no universal latency or reliability guarantee. Primary report: https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03367
- Least-to-most prompting is a reasoning decomposition and sequential-solving method. Parallel independent retrieval and dependent retrieval are separate application topologies. Primary paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.10625
- Step-back prompting abstracts a question to broader principles; the original question and exact constraints still matter. Primary paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06117
- HyDE uses generated passages and an unsupervised contrastive encoder without new relevance-label training. The authors' dense-bottleneck explanation is a hypothesis, not a mechanism guaranteed to remove false details. There is no demonstrated universal query/document subspace split. Primary paper, method and Tables 1/4: https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.99.pdf
- HyDE's reported DL19 nDCG@10 values are Contriever 44.5 and InstructGPT-based HyDE 61.3; DL20 values are 42.1 and 57.9. These historical ranking scores are not answer accuracy or a current product comparison. Source: https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.99.pdf
- Self-RAG's critique reward is linear in family-normalized probabilities. Support includes half-weight partial support; utility uses weights [-1,-0.5,0,0.5,1]. Logging utility is invalid at zero or negative values. Defaults reported in the paper are relevance/support/utility 1/1/0.5 and segment beam width 2. Primary paper, equations 3/4 and Appendix A.3: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.11511
- Released short-form scoring optionally adds exp(mean token log probability); critique-only scoring is also supported. Read scoring lines 100–157 directly. The article's authored scorer isolates critique and doesn't reproduce beam decoding. Pinned implementation: https://github.com/AkariAsai/self-rag/blob/1fcdc420e48f50a7d7ab1ece5494221b93252e99/retrieval_lm/run_short_form.py
- Self-RAG reflection tokens are model predictions. Relevance isn't automatically a hard filter; support isn't verified truth; utility isn't a calibrated helpfulness probability. Training an offline critic to label examples is distinct from hosting that critic at inference. Primary paper: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.11511
- CRAG uses a trained T5-large evaluator, dataset-specific thresholds, and refinement. Correct requires any score above the upper threshold; Incorrect requires every score below the lower threshold; other sets are Ambiguous. Ambiguous retains refined internal evidence and adds external evidence. These labels don't prove source truth or complete coverage. Primary paper, Sections 4.2–4.5 and Appendix B.3: https://arxiv.org/html/2401.15884v3
- CRAG's knowledge strips have variable sentence counts; short one- or two-sentence passages remain one strip. The reported filtering threshold is -0.5. The article's keyword splitter only sketches routing, caps strip count rather than tokens, preserves source IDs, and denies unapproved or blank public queries. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2401.15884v3
- IRCoT interleaves retrieval with intermediate reasoning to drive later searches. A guessed first-hop entity propagates uncertainty rather than establishing evidence. Primary paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10509
