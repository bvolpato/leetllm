# GraphRAG manual evidence review

Checked September 22, 2026. Full article, metadata, questions, downloadable lab, three illustration sources, and banner read individually. No automatic editorial rewrite or model-based review was used.

- Original GraphRAG paper: https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16130v2 . Sections 3.1.3–3.1.6 describe exact-string entity matching, recursive subdivisions, report construction, and map-reduce. Table 2 has 34 podcast and 55 news Level-0 communities. A Level-0 community is not necessarily the entire corpus. LLM-judge preferences don't establish exact incident frequencies or factual accuracy.
- Default dataflow: https://microsoft.github.io/graphrag/index/default_dataflow/ . Title/type entity merges and summarized descriptions don't provide verified alias identity or contradiction resolution. Standard relation descriptions and weights differ from this lesson's custom directed relation labels.
- Indexing methods: https://microsoft.github.io/graphrag/index/methods/ . FastGraphRAG uses NLP noun phrases and co-occurrence relationships and still uses LLM reports. The roughly 75% graph-extraction cost share is Microsoft's estimate, not a corpus-independent allocation.
- Leiden paper: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-41695-z . Louvain may produce disconnected communities. Leiden's refinement gives connectedness guarantees under the analyzed assumptions; stronger guarantees depend on iteration. It can optimize modularity or CPM. These are structural guarantees, not evidence of a true shared topic or cause.
- Inspected clustering input: https://github.com/microsoft/graphrag/blob/82b87bf0434fc69f857663280e2c36a1c48e52fe/packages/graphrag/graphrag/index/operations/cluster_graph.py . Undirected endpoint normalization, pair deduplication, weights, optional largest-connected-component selection, and root parent -1.
- Inspected objective wrapper: https://github.com/microsoft/graphrag/blob/82b87bf0434fc69f857663280e2c36a1c48e52fe/packages/graphrag/graphrag/graphs/hierarchical_leiden.py . Chooses modularity with resolution 1.0. The displayed unweighted equation isn't the full weighted production calculation. Expected adjacency is not an edge probability bounded by 1.
- Default report prompt: https://github.com/microsoft/graphrag/blob/82b87bf0434fc69f857663280e2c36a1c48e52fe/packages/graphrag/graphrag/prompts/index/community_report.py . Generated title, summary, impact rating, rating explanation, and findings with graph-record references. Rating is a model judgment, not calibrated outage severity, query relevance, or frequency. Resolve graph references to source text before judging support.
- Global context builder: https://github.com/microsoft/graphrag/blob/82b87bf0434fc69f857663280e2c36a1c48e52fe/packages/graphrag/graphrag/query/structured_search/global_search/community_context.py . Context uses filters and bounded report batches; map calls aren't necessarily one per report.
- Dynamic selection code: https://github.com/microsoft/graphrag/blob/82b87bf0434fc69f857663280e2c36a1c48e52fe/packages/graphrag/graphrag/query/context_builder/dynamic_community_selection.py . Starts from Level-0 communities and rates query relevance, with fallback behavior when nothing is relevant. The new simple leaf-selection fixture doesn't implement that recovery behavior.
- Dynamic-search experiment, November 15, 2024: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/graphrag-improving-global-search-via-dynamic-community-selection/ . Level 1: 50 global AP News questions, 77% lower average token cost, no significant difference on reported judge criteria. Deeper Table-1 quality comparisons: selected 29-query subset, 58.8% comprehensiveness win rate; 34% higher average cost. No fixed saving follows for this incident corpus.
- Local search: https://microsoft.github.io/graphrag/query/local_search/ . Semantic entity retrieval plus ranked mixed context, not a guaranteed directed two-hop walk.
- DRIFT: https://microsoft.github.io/graphrag/query/drift_search/ . Community primer and iterative local-search follow-ups; describing it as two stages or only explicit graph traversals is misleading.
- LazyGraphRAG, November 25, 2024: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/lazygraphrag-setting-a-new-standard-for-quality-and-cost/ . NLP concept graph and deferred LLM work. Reported 0.1% indexing cost and runtime comparisons are experiment-specific. 100 synthetic questions over 5,590 AP news articles; judge criteria aren't direct answer accuracy. The June 2025 product note is historical availability evidence, not a current open-source CLI feature contract.
- Current local product: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-arc/agents-tools-foundry-local/overview . Agentic Retrieval in Foundry Local is marked preview. The former edge-rag overview redirects here. This product contract should not be confused with a research algorithm or the open-source GraphRAG CLI.
- LightRAG, latest paper v3 April 28, 2025: https://arxiv.org/html/2410.05779v3 . Graph-record profiles and low/high-level query keywords target entities and relationships. No separate GraphRAG-like hierarchy of theme reports. New-document union updates aren't proof of correct deletion, revocation, or conflict handling.
- Update and output contracts: https://microsoft.github.io/graphrag/cli/ and https://microsoft.github.io/graphrag/index/outputs/ . Explicit update methods and table artifacts don't imply automatic source-lifecycle or enterprise-authorization enforcement.

## Teaching boundaries

The downloadable lab uses supplied records, annotated relationships, lexical overlap, and a directed expansion policy. No embedding model, LLM extraction, Leiden optimization, or generated answer was executed. Policy use doesn't imply a policy defect or prove causality; edge labels now state `uses_policy` and `migration_blocked_by`.

Report admission requires access to every contributing source at its current version. Mixed ACL labels can be safe for a caller authorized for all sources. Derived permissions use their audience intersection, and transitive source/version dependencies must remain enforced while rebuilding. The fixture checks group membership and source hashes; it is not a complete authentication or policy service.

Illustrations use readable themed text, two community roots, authored relevance scores, and the lexical fixture's actual term counts. No invented cosine measurements or universal quality/cost leaderboard remains.
