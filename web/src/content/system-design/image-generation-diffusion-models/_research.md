## Corrections and teaching decisions

- The DiT record named Chen as Peebles's coauthor. The paper names Saining Xie. Corrected that author and DDIM's published year to ICLR 2021 while preserving its 2020 preprint citation key.
- Added the DDPM conditional-posterior mean/variance and a two-point clean-data example. At the stated observation, the marginal reverse distribution mixes two Gaussians with means ±sqrt(2)/3 and variance 1/3; total variance is 5/9. Knowing the clean value removes that extra uncertainty. This calculation is illustrative, not a trained image-model result.
- The text-canvas fixture originally only filled masks. It now changes its last token from “later” to “first”; the native figure marks this revision in amber. The exercise is a fixed update plan, not DiffusionGemma inference. The UI question asks when provisional text may become immutable output.
- Confidence and stable predictions are stopping signals, not factual verification. Distinguish time to first committed text from aggregate token rate and completion time.
- Reproduced three release-fixture mistakes: a string "false" counted as manifest commitment, float attempt 2.0 matched integer 2, and equal list values counted as digests. Require exact boolean/integer representation and matching nonempty string digests. Real publication still needs trusted records, computed digests, and an atomic current-attempt check.
- Added DDIM's eta parameter for a selected reverse jump and kept initial-state/hardware reproducibility separate. Zero eta removes injected noise; the clean endpoint can have zero noise even at positive eta.
- Qualified masked-token text diffusion as one branch. Continuous Diffusion-LM denoises Gaussian vectors into word vectors. The data representation determines which corruption and sampler rules apply.
- Clarified original LDM's VQ regularization with quantization inside the decoder: the diffusion state remains continuous encoder output. This differs from diffusing code IDs.
- Scoped DiT's original FID result to class-conditioned ImageNet generation. It does not measure this campaign generator's prompt adherence or safety. Changed “exact pose control” to external pose control.
- A reviewer can approve a labeled synthetic illustration; approval cannot make generated charts into observed measurements. Removed repeated setup paragraphs manually, keeping the worked math, routes, checkpoints, controls, and release trace.

## Primary selections checked

- [DDPM](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.11239): forward marginals, Gaussian approximation, tractable posterior equations 6–7, noise parameterization, training/sampling algorithms, and original 1,000-step linear schedule. [Sohl-Dickstein et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.03585): complete abstract and metadata for the historical forward/reverse construction.
- [Progressive distillation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.00512): velocity parameterization and inverse, successive step halving, and the limits of low-step results. Diffusion v is not automatically a straight-path flow-matching velocity.
- [CFG](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2207.12598): conditional dropout, two predictions, paper versus common-library scale conventions, guidance/diversity tradeoff, and tested dropout rates. [Stable Diffusion API](https://huggingface.co/docs/diffusers/api/pipelines/stable_diffusion/text2img): guidance enabled above 1, negative-prompt behavior, and DDIM-specific eta.
- [DDIM](https://arxiv.org/html/2010.02502v4): shared per-timestep marginals, non-Markov forward family, sparse schedules, deterministic setting, eta formula, and finite-step reconstruction error. Deterministic sampling does not guarantee exact inversion.
- [Latent diffusion publisher PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/papers/Rombach_High-Resolution_Image_Synthesis_With_Latent_Diffusion_Models_CVPR_2022_paper.pdf): sections 3.1–3.3 on KL/VQ regularization, continuous latent diffusion, and cross-attention. Web PDF parsing failed; downloaded the PDF and directly read these text selections. No claim to have reviewed the entire supplement.
- [DiT](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.09748v2): author metadata, patching, adaLN/adaLN-Zero, class-conditioned setup, latent shapes, and FID comparisons. [Stable Diffusion 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.03206v1): conditional flow averaging, rectified-flow timestep sampling, MM-DiT joint attention with separate weights, and 16-channel latents.
- [Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02747), [Rectified Flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.03003), [D3PM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03006), [Diffusion-LM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14217), and [VQ-VAE](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00937): complete abstracts/metadata for training/path, discrete-state, continuous-word-vector, and codebook distinctions. No newest-model performance inferred from these historical papers.
- [DiffusionGemma guide](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/diffusiongemma) and [model card](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/diffusiongemma/model_card): complete published pages, experimental status, supported inputs, 25.2B/3.8B parameters, expert counts, encoder/decoder cache architecture, 256-token canvas, 48-step maximum, adaptive stopping, and low-to-medium batch advantage. Model and sampler were not run.
- [DPM-Solver](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.00927), [consistency models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01469), [LCM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.04378), [LCM-LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.05556), and [ADD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.17042): complete abstracts/metadata; consistency method's ODE-trajectory mapping read separately. Low-step claims stay scoped to trained models, compatible schedules, and reported experiments.
- [ControlNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05543): complete abstract/metadata for frozen backbone and zero-initialized control branches. Stable LoRA/FlashAttention mechanisms reuse preceding manual reviews; no new package/version or performance guarantee added.
- [Diffusers reproducibility](https://huggingface.co/docs/diffusers/using-diffusers/reusing_seeds): generator state, CPU/GPU randomness, deterministic-algorithm limits. [SynthID](https://deepmind.google/models/synthid/): published image-watermark/detection selections. Robustness to common edits is not a cryptographic origin guarantee, nor proof that a negative detector result means human creation.
