# Manual source notes, September 22, 2026

- Read the complete original article, twelve marked examples, ten questions, ten reference records, generated research, four native sources, Mermaid, and banner source. Replaced unsupported interconnect timings, deployment claims, and blanket strategy rules.
- [Qwen model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B) gives approximate 35B total/3B active parameters and an eight-GPU 262,144-context launch example. It doesn't prove that degree is required. [Released configuration](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B/blob/main/config.json) has ten full-attention and thirty linear-attention layers; full-attention heads are 16 Q/2 KV, dimension 256. Hybrid state isn't a forty-layer full-history KV array.
- The 70 GB weight payload, 80 decimal GB budget, and 16 GB other/reserve are rounded arithmetic assumptions, not observed allocations or a universal reserve. Active parameters proxy part of compute, not total FLOPs or resident weights. Offload and replicated tensors change placement. Removed incorrect unexplained native concurrency estimates.
- [vLLM scaling guide](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/main/docs/serving/parallelism_scaling.md) explicitly permits cross-node TP and discusses intra-node PP for uneven splits or GPUs without NVLink. Its recommendations are candidates to validate. [GB200 NVL72](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/gb200-nvl72/) has a 72-GPU NVLink domain; eight is not a universal scale-up limit. No headline vendor performance ratio is adopted.
- [Megatron section 3](https://arxiv.org/html/1909.08053) supports column/row pairing and two in-layer all-reduces in the classic dense forward. Embedding/output handling adds communication; sequence/context/expert/fused layouts can change it. Floating-point reduction ordering can change numerics. Fixed native alt text confusing output shape [1,2] with actual values [-2.6585,8.3411]. Pinned the CPU tensor lab to torch 2.14.0.
- [Current QKV layer](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/main/vllm/model_executor/layers/linear.py) partitions query heads and replicates KV projections when TP is a compatible multiple of the smaller KV-head count. Unchanged per-head history doesn't imply unchanged pool capacity: weight savings can enlarge the pool. Checked the relevant constructor and rank-index branch, not the full module.
- [NCCL collectives](https://docs.nvidia.com/deeplearning/nccl/user-guide/docs/usage/collectives.html) explains sum all-reduce and other primitives. [NCCL tests reporting](https://github.com/NVIDIA/nccl-tests/blob/master/doc/PERFORMANCE.md) separates small-message latency, algorithm bandwidth, and corrected bus bandwidth. Startup assumptions of 5/20 us and sustained send bandwidth of 300 GB/s aren't measured interconnect results or guaranteed exposed latency.
- Derived the finite ideal pipeline occupancy from equal-duration stage-slots. A dimensionless fill/drain ratio isn't elapsed time or a full-model latency multiplier. The utilization helper accepted fractional counts before correction; its continuation fails before integer/Boolean guards and passes afterward.
- [Llama architecture](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.21783v3) and [DeepSeek-V3 abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.19437) support the 405B/671B names. Their raw half-byte estimates are 202.5/335.5 decimal GB, below the assumed eight-device 640 GB sum. This refutes a blanket raw-weight impossibility without validating actual INT4 support, quality, scales, placement, or KV headroom.
- [Megatron strategies](https://docs.nvidia.com/megatron-core/developer-guide/latest/user-guide/parallelism-guide.html) distinguishes LayerNorm/Dropout sequence partitioning from context partitioning. [Ring Attention section 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.01889) combines online statistics while moving KV blocks; complete communication hiding has compute/bandwidth conditions. C visited KV blocks imply C−1 shifts in the simple noncausal layout.
- [Ulysses design](https://github.com/deepspeedai/DeepSpeed/blob/master/blogs/deepspeed-ulysses/README.md) projects local sequence slices into QKV **before** all-to-all head redistribution. It was introduced for training, not as a universal serving decode-cache contract. The simple nonduplicating GQA layout must account for KV as well as query heads.
- [vLLM context guide](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/main/docs/serving/context_parallel_deployment.md) distinguishes prefill from supported decode history partitioning inside existing TP ranks. Logical dispatch/combine isn't necessarily two literal all-to-all calls. Redundant experts and skew matter; assignment counts aren't latency measurements.
