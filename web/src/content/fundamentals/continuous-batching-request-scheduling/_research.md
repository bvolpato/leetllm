# Manual source notes, September 22, 2026

- Read this bundle individually, including all examples, questions, five native figures, banner sources, and thirteen original reference records. Numerical CPU traces are authored teaching fixtures, not serving measurements.
- [Orca](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi22/presentation/yu) introduces iteration-level scheduling and selective batching. Its historical speedup includes both techniques; iteration scheduling does not require PagedAttention.
- [Current vLLM optimization source](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/main/docs/configuration/optimization.md) documents decode-first chunked prefill and default recompute preemption. [V1 guide](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/main/docs/usage/v1_guide.md) supports FCFS and priority scheduling. [Request queue](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/main/vllm/v1/core/sched/request_queue.py) uses smaller priorities first, with arrival-time tie-breaking. The CPU fixture explicitly uses the opposite convention.
- [Qwen3.6-27B configuration](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B/blob/main/config.json) has sixteen full-attention layers, four KV heads, and dimension 256. At FP16, growing attention KV is 64 KiB/token and 0.25 GiB/4,096 positions. Recurrent state, page rounding, and workspace are additional.
- Distinguish original [SARATHI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.16369) from [Sarathi-Serve, OSDI 2024](https://www.usenix.org/conference/osdi24/presentation/agrawal). Stall-free admission keeps ongoing decodes scheduled; it does not establish constant iteration duration or guaranteed ITL attainment. Later chunks attend to longer history.
- [DistServe](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.09670) evaluates OPT models on A100s with a 25 Gbit/s inter-node network and placement exploiting NVLink. Its up-to-7.4x request-rate result versus DeepSpeed-MII is workload/SLO-specific. Disaggregation does not universally require 400/800 Gbit/s links or fixed prefill/decode TP degrees.
- [Mooncake](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.00079) describes a distributed KV-cache-centric design using CPU/DRAM/SSD storage and state-transfer mechanisms. Fixed 190 GB/s and 5 ms handoff figures were unsupported in this bundle and removed. Corrected the first author's initial to Ruoyu Qin.
- [Current vLLM disaggregation source](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/blob/main/docs/features/disagg_prefill.md) marks the feature experimental, states it does not improve throughput by itself, and lists compatible connectors. Optional KV offloading is distinct from legacy swap preemption.
- Payload divided by payload bandwidth is a serialization lower bound. Added decimal 10/25 Gbit/s conversions and a delivery-policy trace: early first-token arrival can coexist with a long next-token gap. The trace's queue and stage durations are assumed, not observed.
- [MLPerf rules](https://github.com/mlcommons/inference_policies/blob/master/inference_rules.adoc) use scheduled-query latency and defined arrivals for relevant scenarios. This article's hand-picked arithmetic and proposed open-loop sweep do not claim MLPerf compliance. [Etalon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07000) motivates measuring stream fluidity beyond averages.
- [SGLang](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang) and [TensorRT-LLM attention](https://nvidia.github.io/TensorRT-LLM/features/attention.html) confirm the table's named scheduling features. These are mechanisms to compare, not universal engine recommendations. Relevant passages inspected, not full upstream repositories or manuals.
