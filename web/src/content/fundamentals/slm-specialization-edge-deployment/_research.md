# Manual review notes: September 22, 2026

- Scope: an inspection terminal workshop with explicitly hypothetical quality, latency, power, and bandwidth budgets. No mobile inference, device soak, energy measurement, delegate trace, privacy boundary test, or OTA recovery experiment was performed.
- SLM has no universal size cutoff. Specialization changes behavior through training/data/retrieval/constraints; it is not necessarily parameter pruning or removal of unrelated knowledge.
- Decode sizing is conditional on active matrix weights and DRAM reads. Stored embedding tables, expert sparsity, caches, and reuse invalidate every-parameter streaming. Integer TOPS and floating-point FLOPs are not interchangeable utilization measures. Artifact storage, active traffic, cache, workspace, and process peaks remain separate budgets.
- MobileLLM's relevant architectural ablations and on-device section: https://arxiv.org/html/2402.14905v2 . Approximately 125M/350M designs favor depth, tying, and GQA. Sharing reduced one 135M design to 119M; immediate layer reuse adds compute and state despite potential locality. Its iPhone 13 FP16/MPS execution averaged 15.6 versus 16.0 ms over 50 iterations. The tested Llama-v2 7B distillation was slower and comparable/inferior in accuracy, not a guaranteed improvement. Relevant selections only, not an entire paper audit.
- Hinton exact gradient and centered high-temperature approximation: https://arxiv.org/html/1503.02531v1 . Unscaled forward-KL gradient is (p_student - p_teacher)/tau; the approximate squared shrinkage includes temperature's effect on the probability difference. Scaling by tau² is approximate compensation, not universal stability. CPU finite-difference fixture verifies the exact expression at three temperatures.
- Phi-3 training-method passages: https://arxiv.org/html/2404.14219v4 . Filtered public and synthetic data are combined; this is not an all-synthetic requirement. Domain splits, applicability, unanswerable cases, and held-out evaluation remain necessary. Schema, runtime assertions, or citation substrings cannot guarantee truth or flawless learned behavior.
- MiniLLM's actual title and reverse-KL/on-policy method: https://arxiv.org/html/2306.08543v5 . Relevant sections include variance reduction, teacher-mixed sampling, length normalization, and mode-seeking tradeoffs. It is not just substituting a KL argument order in the forward objective.
- Phi-4-mini-instruct model/training sections: https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-4-mini-instruct . Dense 3.8B, GQA, tied embeddings, 128K context, filtered/public/educational plus synthetic training data. A context capability does not prove a handheld can allocate or serve it.
- Gemma 4 architecture table: https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/core/model_card_4 . E2B/E4B list 2.3B/4.5B effective and 5.1B/8B including embeddings, text/image/audio support, and encoder components. Effective is not MoE active-expert terminology; inspect the exact artifact and serving allocations.
- Qwen3-1.7B card: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B . 28 layers, 16 query/8 KV heads, 32,768 context, thinking/non-thinking template modes. Thinking work precedes final text and needs its own latency/work/output budget. These models are documented examples, not a current universal ranking.
- ExecuTorch backend overview: https://docs.pytorch.org/executorch/stable/backends-overview.html . Stable docs identify 1.3, target-specific .pte export, and partial delegation/portable CPU fallback. Coverage percentage alone cannot explain performance. Platform memory and partition schedule determine copies, synchronization, and conversions.
- Apple Core ML preparation transcript: https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2024/10159/ . Relevant compression, stateful KV, and SDPA passages distinguish GPU/Neural Engine strengths. Conversion/compressed storage does not guarantee integer NPU computation or whole-model Neural Engine placement. Historical WWDC24 feature introduction, not a claim of latest API additions.
- MLC deployment workflow: https://llm.mlc.ai/docs/get_started/introduction.html . Converted weights, config, and compiled model library are distinct; mobile/WebGPU deployments may use explicit compilation. A single shader is not the entire artifact. Reused preceding bundle's inspected llama.cpp architecture/backend/GGUF notes: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp .
- ORT Mobile flow and provider guidance: https://onnxruntime.ai/docs/tutorials/mobile/ . Model/package/platform-specific support and partition costs must be measured. ORT GenAI's separate preview generation loop: https://onnxruntime.ai/docs/genai/ . Do not transplant all general ORT Mobile providers into a GenAI support claim.
- PyTorch 2.14 capture/dynamism/serialization selections: https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.14/user_guide/torch_compiler/export.html . ExportedProgram round-trip is a CPU graph contract, not a mobile .pte, NPU executable, or placement/performance test. Fixture pins torch 2.14.0 and checks four permitted batches and invalid feature shape.
- Android thermal API device/status passages: https://developer.android.com/games/optimize/adpf/thermal . Status/headroom mappings and support vary; NONE can fail to report actual throttling. Removed universal 3-to-5 W, 85°C junction, 42°C touch, clocks, warm-up, and slowdown percentages. Synthetic rate decline is not a DVFS diagnosis.
- Energy arithmetic: assumed constant whole-system phase powers and non-overlapping phase durations. Decode-only 30,000-token energy is 1.62 or 5 Wh; eight-hour totals including the fixture's prefill/retrieval and idle assumptions are 11.13 or 14.07 Wh. Both being below a nominal 15 Wh is not battery qualification. Corrected the native's erroneous 2.3 mWh/1K label to about 54 at 3.5 W and 18 tokens/s.
- Routing: token entropy/margins describe prediction distributions, not factual accuracy or network authority. Calibrate answer-level risk on held-out data and enforce complete-payload classification outside the model. Route fixture inputs are assumed trusted; no redaction, authorization receipt, network enforcement, or transmission is implemented. Offline, air-gapped, local, and hybrid are distinct conditions.
- Android A/B lifecycle selections: https://source.android.com/docs/core/ota/ab . Active, bootable, and successful states require explicit control and health confirmation. App model recovery needs its own logic, complete assets, session/cache transition, compatible recovery, sufficient storage, and power-loss tests.
- TUF selected metadata checks: https://theupdateframework.github.io/specification/latest/ . Signatures, trusted hashes, version increments, expiry, replay/freeze protection, and key trust are separate checks. A signature does not certify compilation or quality; atomic rename alone does not establish durable activation. Isolated devices need approved offline transfer, not online OTA.
