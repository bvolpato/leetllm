## Editorial notes checked September 22, 2026

Teach the allocation problem through a worked FLOP budget, then distinguish training-only loss optimization from lifetime deployment cost. Require next-token loss, derivatives, and matrix multiplication; don't promise predictability of intelligence or exact extrapolation.

## Historical fits and their limits

- [Kaplan](https://arxiv.org/html/2001.08361) reported empirical power laws and a parameter-heavy frontier. Its single-axis parameter law fits raw loss, without an additive floor. GPT-3's released token/parameter ratio isn't a universal prescription.
- [Chinchilla](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.15556) compares three allocation methods. Approximately balanced compute growth and a roughly 20:1 sizing rule aren't identical statements. For $L=E+A/N^\alpha+B/D^\beta$ and $C\approx6ND$, optimal growth exponents are $\beta/(\alpha+\beta)$ and $\alpha/(\alpha+\beta)$. Equal exponents $\gamma$ give $D/N=(B/A)^{1/\gamma}$, not necessarily 20. The fitted floor is not measured corpus entropy.
- [Porian](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.19146) attributes the reproduced discrepancy mainly to output-head FLOPs, warmup, and optimizer choices. Matching cosine decay alone doesn't establish the explanation; tuned constant-rate experiments recovered approximately balanced allocation.
- [Sardana's ICML 2024 paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/sardana24a.html), by Nikhil Sardana, Jacob Portes, Sasha Doubov, and Jonathan Frankle, incorporates inference at a quality target. Use [the paper's experiments and cost model](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.00448) to explain why inference demand, prompt/output utilization, and extreme-ratio fit errors matter. Longer training isn't guaranteed to improve every task or pay for itself.

## Counting and deployment questions

- Declare parameter counting, token presentations versus unique text, and useful arithmetic versus hardware capacity. The dominant dense-matmul estimate $6ND$ excludes some operations and recomputation; it doesn't determine wall time or dollars.
- In the worked equal-quality 70B/1.4T versus 30B/4T example, the FLOP break-even is 1.65T processed serving tokens. Equal quality requires evaluation; token counts aren't request counts, and per-FLOP prices need not match between training and inference.
- The [original April 2024 Llama 3 8B card](https://huggingface.co/meta-llama/Meta-Llama-3-8B) reports 15T+ tokens. The [Llama 3 paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.21783) reports 15.6T for 405B's initial pre-training. Keep model and release provenance explicit.
- [Villalobos's June 2024 forecast](https://arxiv.org/html/2211.04325v2) projects effective-data utilization, not physical consumption by labs. Its stock is adjusted for quality and useful repetition. Four variants can use the same corpus; their training-token totals aren't necessarily disjoint unique data. [Epoch's September 2025 assessment](https://epoch.ai/publications/what-will-ai-look-like-in-2030) considers synthetic data a possible way past a bottleneck, not evidence of unlimited useful data.
- [Snell](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.03314) studies MATH with PaLM 2-S*, revision, and verifiers. The 14-times-larger comparison is conditional on compute, difficulty, and demand. Longer traces, candidate sampling, and external verifier search are distinct mechanisms.

## Experiments worth teaching

- Make coefficients change the optimum while equal growth stays intact. Keep unitless pedagogical counts separate from historical model measurements.
- Use the [µP paper's linear SGD setup](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.03466) with deterministic test vectors to show width-dependent function updates. Layer and optimizer rules differ; don't publish one universal Adam/SGD recipe. Transfer across width doesn't guarantee every regularization setting or depth change transfers.
- Mark the nine loss points as formula-generated. The lesson supplies three true constants and searches two exponents; this isn't a full five-unknown statistical fit. Two exact anchors can support different floors and forecasts. Include real held-out runs, sensitivity, uncertainty, and a mid-scale loss gate alongside separate throughput profiling.
- [Schaeffer](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.15004) motivates inspecting scoring and sample size. An independent-token model has smooth exact-match probability $p^5$; finite observations can make progress look abrupt. Don't claim every capability obeys a smooth power law or every transition is an artifact.

The illustrations should label normalized allocation multipliers, assumed equal quality, formula-generated points, and extrapolated cells. A worked token-heavy allocation isn't a measured Llama configuration or a proven lifetime optimum. Refit when changing data, tokenizer, optimizer, or architecture; active MoE arithmetic and total weight storage require different accounting.
