Local Vizmatic artwork. Reproducible source: social_banner.tsx.
From the repository's web/ directory, render the 1200x630 PNG at scale 1:

pnpm exec tsx -e "import { renderBanner } from './src/content/preparation/vectors-matrices-tensors/social_banner.tsx'; renderBanner('./src/content/preparation/vectors-matrices-tensors/social_banner.png').then(console.log)"

The diagram uses the lesson's actual H tensor of shape (2, 3, 2). Its two prompt matrices each contain three token rows and two feature columns. H[1, 2] selects [1, 0], removing the batch and token axes and returning a vector of shape (2,). No external image service or upload is required.
